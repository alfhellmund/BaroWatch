BaroWatch

Air pressure monitor for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch

Other languages

BaroWatch shows the current air pressure on your Apple device — measured by the built-in barometer sensor. The app stores data locally and transmits nothing to us or to third parties.

Features

  • Live display of air pressure in hPa, either as station pressure (QFE) or converted to sea level (QNH) — on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch
  • Altitude for the QNH conversion from GPS or as a fixed altitude; calibration against a reference pressure
  • History chart (line, area or bar) with a selectable time range from 1 to 48 hours, or alternatively an analog gauge; a second chart on iPad
  • Statistics cards (iPhone/iPad): minimum, maximum and average of the selected time range
  • Trend arrow (rising, stable, falling) for the last 30 minutes
  • Pressure alarm: separate thresholds for pressure drop and rise; time window and quiet period adjustable
  • Measurement in the background too; interval and storage period (1 hour to 365 days) adjustable
  • Syncing of readings between Apple Watch, iPhone and iPad (iPhone and iPad via a pairing code)
  • Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and complications for Apple Watch
  • Export of readings (JSON)
  • 29 languages: German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Icelandic, Polish, Czech, Hungarian, Croatian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian

Privacy

BaroWatch transmits no data to us or to third parties. The app stores readings and settings on your device and syncs them only between your own devices. All calculations take place on your devices. More in the privacy policy.
